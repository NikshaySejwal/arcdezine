Our Expertise

Architecture & Planning

We provide comprehensive architectural services from concept to completion. Our process includes site analysis, schematic design, design development, construction documentation, and project management. We focus on creating sustainable and contextually relevant designs that stand the test of time.

Interior Design

Our interior design services are tailored to create spaces that are both beautiful and functional. We handle everything from space planning and furniture selection to lighting design and material specification. Our goal is to create a cohesive and personalized environment that reflects your lifestyle.

Nishawe Furniture

With our in-house furniture brand, Nishawe, we design and manufacture custom pieces that perfectly complement our architectural and interior projects. This integrated approach ensures seamless quality control and a truly unique aesthetic for your space, from bespoke sofas to handcrafted tables.
