The Accountability Standard

Built on Written Proof, Not Broken Promises.

Arcdezine was founded in Gurugram to solve the single largest complaint in Indian construction: finger-pointing between independent architects, contractors, and carpenters.

Our Turnkey Philosophy

One Contract. One Point of Contact.

When design and execution are fragmented, clients bear the cost of coordination mistakes, timeline delays, and material markups.

At Arcdezine, every project is anchored by a dedicated site supervisor, a locked materials schedule, and an active variation log. Furthermore, our proprietary custom furniture brand, Nishawe, allows us to build millwork, sectionals, and cabinetry in our own workshop rather than outsourcing to unverified third parties.

No Unapproved Variations: Any scope change requires written sign-off before site execution.

Written Material Grades: We explicitly document plywood grades, laminate thicknesses, and hardware brands.

Phased Milestone Reviews: Clients approve designated stages before progressive payments are drawn.

Snag List Handover: Defects and adjustments are resolved systematically before handover.
