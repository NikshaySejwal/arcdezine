2. Information We Collect

When you submit a project enquiry or feasibility consultation request, we collect:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, phone number / WhatsApp, and email address.
  • Project Scope Details: Project typology (villa, apartment renovation, showroom, furniture), city/location, approximate budget band, and your written requirements message.
  • Attribution Data: Source URL, referring channel, and timestamp to understand how you discovered Arcdezine.

3. How We Use Your Information

We collect your details strictly for professional, project-related purposes:

  • To review architectural, structural, and site feasibility before recommending a site visit.
  • To prepare initial budget bands, written scopes, and material schedule overviews.
  • To communicate with you via WhatsApp, phone, or email regarding your specific project inquiry.
  • To maintain internal project records should you decide to commission Arcdezine.

4. How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

Your project details are stored securely in internal lead management records accessible solely by Arcdezine's project leads and principal architects.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your contact details to third-party telemarketers, brokers, or external advertising networks under any circumstances.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill project evaluation or ongoing contractual commitments. You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your contact records at any time.
  • Opt out of future follow-up communications.

To exercise these rights, email arcdezine27@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" or message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 98996 24693.
